About the Mod


The Interplan combines several features and can be installed on almost all Infinity Engine games.

• A spell mod: over 150 new spells.

• A shop mod: over 100 new items and over 25 new potions/charms.

• A player-focused mod: 85 items, 57 potions and 23 additional spells to find at random.

• A crafting mod (in BG2EE): 50 recipes for combining potions.

• A mod for enthusiasts (in BG2EE): plenty of things to discover, recycle and collect.

You can find the shop:

• In BG2EE, on Waukeen's Promenade (a new area with a map marker).

• In The Black Pits II, in the merchants' enclave.

• In IWDEE, in the yeti cave (through an item found in one of the crates).

It also offers four components:

• "Duplicate some Interplan spells as cleric/druid spells": duplicates around thirty spells from the shop as cleric spells (support spells and a few offensive spells) and druid spells (elemental spells), and adds high-level abilities.

• "Unlimited potion stock": lets you buy as many potions and charms from the shop as you like, with no limit.

• "Unlimited scroll stock": lets you buy as many scrolls from the shop as you like, with no limit. Overpowered when combined with Oops the Mage.

• (EET installations only) "A smuggler sells Interplan items in Beregost": the smuggler appears only at night, behind the Thunderhammer Smithy. Installing this component removes the low-level items from the BG2 shop and makes them available from the smuggler instead. He also sells other items specific to BG1, almost all level 1–4 scrolls, and potions (in limited quantities).

Compatibility


• In theory, there are no incompatibilities: this mod is “green”; it does not modify any base-game file that could interfere with other mods.

• If The Interplan is installed after Sword Coast Stratagems and its “Install Icewind Dale arcane spells” and “Add 9 arcane spells” components, the spells added by The Interplan do not count toward the limit of 50 spells per level.

• Exception: partial incompatibility with AutoDescription . This mod has problems with some Interplan items and does not always display their descriptions accurately. For four items, it even removes the description entirely, making them look as though they do nothing: Blood Mail +2, Dreamdancer's Ring, Every God Ring and Bearfriend.

Notes

• Characters from realities outside D&D are not balanced as they would be in a game and do not always have special abilities. In particular:

• Almost all bards know no spells, but they know more songs thanks to the inclusion of "Bard Song Switching".

• Almost all paladins know no spells and cannot turn undead.

• Almost all rangers know no spells.

• Almost all monks (i.e. martial artists) do not use Stunning Blow or Quivering Palm. They are renamed Brawlers or Karate Fighters.

• Almost all Kensai (manga characters wearing a kimono, without armor, wielding a katana and highly effective in combat) do not use Kai.

• Most mages are sorcerers (many can cast spells spontaneously, without needing to memorize them).

• Mages with elemental summons have them for role-playing reasons, and these summons require no mental combat.

• Some characters have charged items or special abilities. Remember to select them at least once to check.

• You can interact with some characters (the summoning method does not allow their cursor to be changed to "Talk", so you must initiate dialogue yourself): Creeper (buys/sells at full price), Deckard Cain (identifies for free), M'aiq the Liar (silly dialogue).

• Some characters can be useful: Heads-or-Tails (steals items and drops them), Morticia Addams and Gargamel (create potions and drop them), and Ashrian (her healing potion restores all HP to a target within touching distance).

• I'm sorry: this was a lovely project, but it ultimately proved disappointing because of BG2's limited animations. It is impossible to reproduce barbarians' bare chests, old wizards' long beards, ladies' long hair, heroes' cloaks, the strange weapons of "monsters", and so on. There isn't even orange, yellow or pink... So I hope you won't hold it against me too much if a particular character doesn't quite look the way you imagined.

• A reminder of the songs added for bards by "Bard Song Switching":

  • The Ballad of Three Heroes grants +1 to attack rolls, damage and all saving throws.
  • The Tale of Curran Strongheart grants immunity to Fear and Morale Failure.
  • The Song of Kaudies grants immunity to all sound-based effects, such as silence, deafness and power words.
  • Tymora's Melody grants +1 Luck, +3 saving throws, and +10 Lore and thief skills.
  • The Siren's Yearning charms enemies within 10 meters unless they save vs. spells. It does NOT affect mindless enemies such as golems and skeletons.
  • War Chant of Sith grants +2 Armor Class, +10% physical damage resistance, and regenerates 3 HP per round.
  • Enhanced Bard Song grants +4 to attack rolls, damage and Armor Class, +2 to all saving throws, +5% magic resistance, and immunity to Fear, Stun, Confusion and normal weapons. The bard gains an additional +6 Armor Class and +5% magic resistance.

The Magic Cauldron

The shop's map includes a magic cauldron where you can place potions to combine them. There are just a few things you need to know:

1: You must pay 100 gold pieces for each combination. If you do not have enough gold, it will not work. For example, combining 24 potions at once requires 2,400 gold pieces. Keep that in mind!

2: All potions created by the cauldron are standardized: anyone can use them, except Wizard Slayers. They are marked with an * as a reminder.

Here are all the recipes the Cauldron can use:

Healing Potions: (one at a time or in batches of 5, 15 or 24)

Potion of Healing (8 HP) + Amplifier = Potion of Extra Healing (18 HP)

Potion of Healing (8 HP) or Amplifier + Potion of Extra Healing (18 HP) = Potion of Superior Healing (27 HP)

Potion of Healing (8 HP) or Amplifier + Potion of Superior Healing (27 HP) = Potion of Major Healing (40 HP)

Potion of Healing (8 HP) or Amplifier + Potion of Major Healing (40 HP) + Elixir of Health = Morgana's Tear (65 HP and cures poison, disease and fatigue)

Potion of Healing (8 HP) + Powerful Amplifier + Elixir of Health = Morgana's Tear

Potion of Extra Healing (18 HP) + Potion of Superior Healing (27 HP) + Elixir of Health = Morgana's Tear

Potion of Extra Healing (18 HP) + Potion of Major Healing (40 HP) or Powerful Amplifier = Potion of Supreme Healing (60 HP)

Potion of Extra Healing (18 HP) + Potion of Supreme Healing (60 HP) + Elixir of Health = Miracle Cure ("Heal")

Potion of Superior Healing (27 HP) + Morgana's Tear = Miracle Cure

Potion of Major Healing (40 HP) + Powerful Amplifier + Elixir of Health = Miracle Cure

Miracle Cure + Amplifier = Potion of Life (Resurrection on a dead target)

Notes

• You can combine potions in stages. For example, start with Potions of Healing to create Potions of Extra Healing, then add more Potions of Healing to create Potions of Superior Healing, then add still more Potions of Healing to create Potions of Major Healing.

• If you have 48 potions to combine, you can combine 24, then another 24 without closing the cauldron, and collect all 48 at the end.

• You can also mix recipes and create strength potions, healing potions and other potions at the same time.

• Strength potions marked with an * can replace vanilla strength potions in the recipes.

• The thieves' Potion of Frost Giant Strength can replace the Potion of Frost Giant Strength (Strength 21) in the recipes.

• The potions from Arcanum sold by " Todd the Super Tester Guy " are the same as those found in the Discombobulator and can be used in the recipes.
